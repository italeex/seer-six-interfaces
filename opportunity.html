Interface 04 · Opportunity / selection markets

Markets for competitions: who gets selected?

Hackathons, grant rounds, open calls — hundreds of entries, a handful of prizes, months of waiting. Here every submission is a tradable entry: the price is the crowd’s live estimate of selection, and the leaderboard sorts by it. Whoever turns out to be right is paid; nobody has to trust a judge’s whisper.

How a selection market works

  1. Finalists are listed as outcomes by anyone — the list mirrors the official shortlist.
  2. Each entry trades between 0 and 100% of a prize.
  3. When organizers publish results, winning entries settle at 100%.
