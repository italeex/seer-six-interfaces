Interface 02 · Deep / many-outcome & agent markets

Markets for hundreds of outcomes — built for agents.

Some questions don’t fit in yes/no. Deep markets carry a whole outcome space — score bands over a field of 50 grant proposals, a ranking of 50 teams, a distribution of reviewer scores. They are the markets AI agents were built to trade: numeric outcome IDs, JSON metadata, API-first ordering.
