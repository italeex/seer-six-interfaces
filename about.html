About this design concept.

Seven pages, one identity: a working prototype of how Seer’s six interfaces could look, read and behave as a single product.

Rationale

Why it looks like this

Seer is a protocol, not an app — so the design puts the shared core first and treats the six interfaces as windows onto the same book of markets: one header, one table grammar, one palette, and numbers always in monospace so prices stay comparable at a glance. A dark surface with two accents (violet for structure, cyan for data) lets probability, volume and risk carry the colour, and the restrained glow keeps attention on the few elements that ask for action. Everything is plain HTML, one CSS file and vanilla JS — no build step — because a concept should be readable, forkable and able to run from a branch with a click.

Not a product

Disclaimer

This is an independent design concept for the Seer design bounty on poidh.xyz (bounty #38). It is not affiliated with, produced by, or endorsed by Seer. No wallet is connected, no API is called, and nothing here can place a trade. All market titles, outcome sets, prices, volumes, liquidity figures, proposals, entries and agents are invented sample data chosen to look plausible — they are not real market data, not advice, and not a statement about any real protocol, team or program. Third-party names that appear in the risk comparison are used only as familiar labels for a hypothetical interface.

Brand kit

Departures from the Electric Foundation kit

We could not open the Figma brand kit — it was not accessible to this build — so nothing here is claimed to implement it. The identity below is an original interpretation of the brief, and these are the deliberate departures:

  • Palette. A dark instrument-panel scheme — #08090C background, violet #7A5BFF and cyan #38E8FF accents — chosen for data density and long reading sessions, not taken from any kit token we could see.
  • Typography. System font stack with a monospace face for every number. No custom or commercial webfont is loaded, since the build deliberately ships with zero external requests.
  • Logo. A plain geometric mark and a letterspaced wordmark set in type; no asset from the kit is reproduced.
  • Components. A house grammar of 12px cards, pill statuses, tabbed panels and table-first layouts, built from scratch rather than matched to kit components.
  • Imagery. No illustrations, photography or icon library; the only graphics are inline SVG charts drawn at runtime from the sample data.
  • Unknown unknowns. Spacing scale, motion rules, voice and illustration style from the kit are unreviewed here; if the judging panel wants kit alignment, this concept is structured to be re-skinned without touching page structure.

The six interfaces

  1. Home — the protocol beneath the applications, and the whole ecosystem on one page.
  2. App — the explorer: filterable market table, detail panel, inline-SVG price chart and order book.
  3. Deep — many-outcome markets, including an agent market over 50 grant proposals.
  4. Futarchy — pass-vs-fail conditional markets on a success metric, with a decision summary.
  5. Opportunity — selection markets and a leaderboard of entries with implied probabilities.
  6. Risk — protocol risk estimates, comparison table, ranges and a chart.
