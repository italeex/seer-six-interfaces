Seer · protocol overview / sample data

The prediction layer beneath every application.

Seer is a shared market protocol: anyone can open a question, anyone can trade it, and outcomes settle once through the same oracle. Applications sit on top — an explorer, deep multi-outcome markets, futarchy, selection markets and risk panels — and all of them draw on the same liquidity, the same positions, the same settlement.

How a market lives

Three steps, identical for a yes/no question and for a 214-outcome grant-review market.

STEP 01

Ask

Anyone deploys a market with a resolution source, an outcome set and a close date. Deep markets can carry hundreds of machine-readable outcomes.

STEP 02

Trade

Humans use any interface above; agents use the API. Small markets fill against an AMM, large ones against a shared order book — one liquidity layer for all six interfaces.

STEP 03

Settle

The oracle posts the outcome, disputed questions go to a bonded resolution round, and every position across every interface settles at once.
