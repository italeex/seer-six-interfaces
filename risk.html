Interface 05 · Risk / adverse-event markets

Where the market prices protocol risk.

Every protocol gets a family of adverse-event markets — an exploit, a depeg, an outage, a slashing. The price is a live, tradeable estimate of risk that can be compared across protocols on one scale, instead of a rating that updates quarterly.

What moves these markets

  • Exploit disclosures and live incident trackers
  • Audit results and upgrade announcements
  • Governance votes on collateral and parameters
  • TVL concentration and oracle dependency changes

Risk ranges · estimated interval vs point estimate

One scale, every protocol

Each bar spans the market’s estimated interval (low to high confidence); the dot is the current price. Wider bars mean the market disagrees with itself more.
