Interface 03 · Futarchy / conditional governance

Conditional markets that decide what to do next.

Each proposal is paired with a success metric and two conditional markets: one prices the metric if the proposal passes, one prices it if the proposal fails. Neither prices the vote itself — the gap between the two forecasts is the decision signal, and anything inside the noise band goes back for revision instead of being enacted.
